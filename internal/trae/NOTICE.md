# Trae protocol references

The `auth` and `upstream` packages are adapted from
[mmqz/cpa-multi-plugins](https://github.com/mmqz/cpa-multi-plugins), commit
`2f8193a961d80eb1ea51ad8b5e6a5d8669947acc`, copyright 2026 mmqz,
under the MIT license reproduced in `LICENSE`.

That project identifies Sliverkiss/traework2api as the source of its domestic
SOLO protocol. This notice records the implementation provenance; protocol
behavior was also compared with connectedGraph/trae2api-web and buddy-proxy.
Gateway orchestration, authorization and management remain work2api code.

Desktop storage decoding and the domestic OAuth handoff sentinel follow buddy-proxy, commit 4858cb8c22213ee17e2256b1d91d6ce67f646cdd, copyright (c) 2026 Mayer (MIT; license also reproduced in LICENSE).
